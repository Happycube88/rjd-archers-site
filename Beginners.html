Understanding Bow Types

During your course, you will primarily shoot a Barebow or standard beginner recurve setup. Here is a quick look at the main styles you will see on the line:

Barebow / Starter Recurve

The ideal starting point. Features a handle (riser), flexible limbs, a string, and an arrow rest. Barebow shoots without sights or stabilizers, relying on instinctive or gap-shooting techniques.

Compound Bow

Uses a system of cables and eccentric cams. It offers a mechanical advantage, letting you hold a lower draw weight at full draw while delivering high arrow speed and precision scopes.

Traditional Longbow

A classic D-shape wooden bow steeped in history. Shoots wooden arrows straight off the hand with minimal accessories, demanding traditional touch and form.

Try Before You Buy & Beginner Gear Breakdown

The "Try Before You Buy" Golden Rule:

Please do not buy any equipment before starting your course! Archery equipment must be correctly matched to your physical draw length, hand size, and physical draw weight. Our coaches will lend you everything during the course and advise you on exactly what to purchase when you are ready.

Safety & Dress Code (Archery GB Rules)

Because safety is our absolute priority on the shooting line, all beginners and members must adhere to standard Archery GB dress and safety guidelines:

  • Footwear: Must be closed-toe shoes (sturdy trainers, walking shoes, or flat boots). Strictly no sandals, flip-flops, Crocs, or open-toe footwear.
  • Clothing: No loose or baggy clothing, dangling jewelry, or scarves that could catch on a bowstring. Combat trousers with loose dangling side pockets are not acceptable on the line.
  • Hair & Accessories: Long hair must be tied back securely.
