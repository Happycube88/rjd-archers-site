Beginner's Courses

Start your archery journey with our structured, Archery GB-aligned training courses.

Course Structure & Schedule

Beginner's courses are run on an as-required basis, up to 4 times a year. They typically consist of a structured sequence of training slots (usually 4 sessions) designed to teach you range safety, proper shooting form, and equipment handling under the guidance of our qualified coaches.

Next Upcoming Course:

Saturdays in April (indoor at Llantrisant Leisure Centre). All equipment is fully provided by the club for the duration of the course.

Safety & Dress Code (Archery GB Rules)

Because safety is our absolute priority on the shooting line, all beginners and members must adhere to standard Archery GB dress and safety guidelines:

  • Footwear: Must be closed-toe shoes (no sandals, flip-flops, or open-toe footwear). Sturdy trainers or flat boots are ideal.
  • Clothing: No loose or baggy clothing, dangling jewelry, or scarves that could catch on a bowstring. Combat trousers with loose dangling side pockets are not acceptable on the line.
  • Hair & Accessories: Long hair must be tied back securely.

Fees & How to Join the Waiting List

Courses are paid upfront for the full set of training slots. Spaces are limited to ensure safe line supervision and quality coaching. To inquire about availability or join our waiting list, please contact the club.
