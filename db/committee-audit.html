Audit log

A permanent record of sensitive changes: roles, membership status, beginner decisions, email changes, club nights, tournaments and entries, club records, 252 scores and badges, one-off guests, responses to website messages and enquiries, backups and purges. Nobody can edit or delete an entry, not even the Chair. Only the Chair, Secretary and Membership roles can read it. It holds no email addresses or other personal details, only who did what to whom, and when.
