Download a backup

Saves a copy of the club's data (members, parents, records, 252 scores, tournaments, entries, club nights, bookings, guests, enquiries, messages) as a file on this computer. Only the Chair, Secretary and Membership roles can do this. Do it at least once a month and after any big change. The date of the last backup is recorded and shown here and in the Committee area.

Handle with care. The file holds names, dates of birth, phone numbers and children's details. Keep it on an encrypted or password-protected drive, never email it, and delete old copies you no longer need.

    The file is locked on this computer before it is saved. If the passphrase is lost the backup cannot be opened, so share it with a second committee member in a safe way.
