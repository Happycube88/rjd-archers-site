Purge old personal data

For people who have left or lapsed, and beginners the committee declined. A purge removes their name, address, phone, email, date of birth, next of kin, sign-in, parent links, 252 scores, and any enquiries or website messages from their email address. It keeps tournament fees, booking counts and, unless you choose otherwise, their name on the club records. It cannot be undone, so take a backup first if you are unsure. Only the Chair or Secretary can do this.

People who can be purged

Mark someone as Left or Lapsed on Edit people first. Anyone holding a committee role must have it removed before they can be marked as left.
