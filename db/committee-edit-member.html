Email change requests

Members cannot change their own email address. Check that the request is genuine (you know the person, or have asked them) before approving. Approving removes their old sign-in: they sign in with the new address and set up 2FA again. The Chair, Secretary or Membership can decide; nobody can approve their own.

    Roles (take care: these give access to everyone's details)

    Chair, Secretary, Membership, Welfare, Treasurer and Tournaments give committee access, including the member list and everyone's details. Record keeper (records and 252 scores) and Club night setup (club night lists) do not. Only the Chair and Secretary can change roles, status and beginner decisions or make someone a member. There must always be a Chair or Secretary.

    Membership status (Chair or Secretary)

    Only active people can book club nights or enter tournaments. Remove any committee roles before marking someone as left. Left or lapsed people can then be purged on the Purge page.

    After the beginners course

    Inviting needs the course proof recorded above. An invited beginner is made a member on the Add people page. A declined person stays on the list so a repeat application is spotted; the website never tells them why.
