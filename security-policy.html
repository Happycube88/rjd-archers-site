Security and reporting a problem

RJD Archers looks after members' details with care, including children's details. If you think you have found a weakness in this website, we would like to hear about it.

How to tell us

Email rjdarchers.members@gmail.com with what you found, where, and how to repeat it. We will reply as soon as a volunteer can, usually within a few days.

Please

  • Do not look at, change or download anyone else's personal details.
  • Do not overload the site or send spam through the forms.
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before telling anyone else.

We are a volunteer club and cannot pay rewards, but we will thank you and, if you wish, say so publicly once it is fixed.
