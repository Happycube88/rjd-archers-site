Annual Club Membership & Affiliations

Your annual membership fee is all-inclusive, covering mandatory regional and national governing body affiliations—specifically Archery GB (the national governing body), Wales Archery, and Glamorgan Archery—to ensure full insurance validity and competition eligibility. A portion is also allocated directly to the club to help maintain facilities, equipment upkeep (bosses, targets, and spare gear), and venue administration.

Weekly Club Nights & Special Events

Regular Attendance

Club Nights (£2.50 per session)

Held twice a week (indoor during winter, outdoor during summer). Members book their line slots online in advance, contributing a flat £2.50 per player per session to directly help cover venue hire and targets.

Seasonal Fun

Festive Shoots (100% Free!)

Our special holiday club nights near Christmas and Easter feature fun novelty targets, social gatherings, and friendly club celebrations. These seasonal shoots are completely free of charge for all active members.

Tournaments & Competitions

RJD Archers hosts open competitions and club tournaments 3 to 4 times a year. Unlike regular club nights, competition fees are chargeable for all entrants to cover tournament administration, target face wear, and medals/prizes.
