Welcome to RJD Archers

A friendly family club welcoming children over 8 and adults of all skill levels across South Wales.

About Our Club

Beginners are welcome to borrow club equipment, and anyone wishing to continue beyond beginner level is supported to buy the correct equipment suited to their own needs. Our coaches are all Archery GB qualified, offer lots of individual guidance, and are DBS checked.

Welsh Language Provision

Mae hefyd hyfforddwr Cymraeg ar gael os hofech ddysgu yn eich mamiaeth neu hoff iaith. (Training delivery through the medium of Welsh is available upon request).

Where & When We Meet

  • Summer Season: May to mid-September, Tuesdays & Fridays (18:00 – 20:00 or dusk) at The Old Dog Track, Rhydyfelin.
  • Winter Season: Mid-September to May 1st, Tuesdays & Fridays (18:00 – 20:00) at Llantrisant Leisure Centre.
