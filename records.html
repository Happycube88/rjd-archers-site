252 Outdoor Round Badges

  • Shoot 3 dozen (36) arrows at one distance, 9-based scoring (5 zone). The 252 Round has its own score sheet in the score sheets box, or use the first 36 arrows of any outdoor round at a set distance.
  • Score the target for your bow type twice at the same distance and you earn a quiver badge showing the distance and “252”.
  • Badges are available at 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80 and 100 yards. A recurve average of 7 per arrow (252 ÷ 36) is a good sign you are ready for the next distance.
  • The top verified 252 badge scores are club records.
