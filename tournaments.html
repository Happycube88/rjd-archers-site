Fees

Juniors £12, adults £15 per round.

Who can enter

Members of Archery GB or WA associated organisations. Bring your membership card. Juniors must be supervised by a parent or guardian.

Venue

Llantrisant Leisure Centre, Southgate Park, Llantrisant, CF72 8DJ

How to enter

Online

Enter on this website

For members, registered guests, and parents entering a child. Sign in, pick the round and session, and send the entry. Your age group and fee are worked out for you.

The organiser confirms your place first, then you pay. Bank details appear on your entry once the place is confirmed.

On paper

Print it, fill it in, send a photo

  1. Download the entry form for your event below and print it.
  2. Fill it in by hand and sign it. Juniors need a parent or guardian signature.
  3. Take a clear photo or scan of the whole page.
  4. Email the photo to rjdarcherstournaments@gmail.com, or text the organiser if you need help.
  5. We type your entry in for you and confirm your place. Pay after you hear from us.

Archers from other clubs: this is the way to enter.
